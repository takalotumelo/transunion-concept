A platform for African FinTechs to scale with confidence

Africa’s FinTech sector is in a league of its own. Setting the pace for mobile-led innovation, alternative credit models and increasing demand for accessible digital financial services.

As competition intensifies, trust, scale and smart decisions are critical to long-term success.

At TransUnion, we support FinTechs across the growth curve — from early-stage startups building credit lending alternatives to scaling BNPL providers, payment innovators, neobanks and embedded finance platforms.

We partner with you to design and deliver:

  • Robust identity and fraud solutions that enable friction-right onboarding
  • Credit and affordability insights to expand inclusion
  • Alternative data scores to help reach and assess underserved and underbanked consumers
  • API-first tools to test, launch and refine propositions quickly
  • Programmes that promote consumer financial education and responsible lending
  • Help FinTechs meet compliance needs and deliver on their innovation goals
  • Enabling sustainable and profitable FinTech growth across Africa.
