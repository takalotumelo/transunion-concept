Event · 2024 Financial Services Summit

Charting a Course for Sustainable Growth

Get the latest best practices and industry insights.

10 July 2024The Marriott Hotel, 42 The High St, Melrose, Johannesburg

Register Now

About the Summit

TransUnion is pleased to bring you our premier event, the annual Financial Services Summit, to be hosted in Johannesburg on July 10. This information-packed, one-day event aims to share data-driven insights and best practices in the consumer lending industry. Through this year’s theme — Charting a Course for Sustainable Growth — we’ll explore the ever-evolving dynamics of consumer behaviour.

Amid a new normal, South African lenders that understand current market trends and dynamics will be best positioned to identify opportunities for growth as they continue to navigate uncertain and challenging times. Join us as we delve into the lending landscape, tackling crucial questions, such as:

  • How has ongoing financial and credit education driven motivations of various borrowers in South Africa?
  • Which risk strategies can lenders employ to help navigate volatile economic cycles?
  • What are the latest trends across popular credit products like personal loans, credit cards and retail?

The 2024 TransUnion Financial Services Summit promises to deliver insightful, actionable strategies to empower lenders to acquire, manage and retain customers. As we strive to identify progressive ways to drive growth and profitability in the lending ecosystem, we’re confident this event will inspire robust discussion and provide you with innovative ideas for managing your business.
